What this score means

Global score estimates whether this stable release is broadly safe to install. It combines hard gates such as known CVEs, wait/hotfix status, release age, beta shake-out, breaking changes, and the balance of serious issues opened vs fixed during the release window.

My install appears after you select a meaningful profile. It keeps global/core issues, keeps issues for your selected surfaces, and filters out unrelated surfaced issues so the trend reflects your setup instead of everyone else's.

The score is a triage aid, not a guarantee. Expand a release to see the evidence issues behind the number.
